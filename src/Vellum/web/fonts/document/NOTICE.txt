Fonts bundled with Vellum for writing new text in PDFs

Every font here is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1; each family's licence, with its
copyright notice and any Reserved Font Names, is the OFL.txt in its folder. The files are the static
instances Google Fonts distributes for these families, unchanged and under their own names, except Liu San
(below). Vellum
embeds a subset of a font's glyphs in a PDF when text written in it is saved.
